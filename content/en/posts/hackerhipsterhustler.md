## There's a role for everyone at RGUHack
 
### The Hacker
Do you love working with new tools and frameworks? Wanting to code right away? You will be responsible for turning dreams and wireframes into apps and websites, no matter how many bugs you encounter, making sure that the team vision is materialised and (hopefully) running.
 
### The Hipster
Happen to find yourself looking at the resolution of a logo or figuring out the font of a cafe menu for too long? You will make sure that whatever the team makes looks, feels and sounds as awesome as possible with your artistic touch. Give that much-needed WOW factor to it all.
 
### The Hustler
Thinking of all the ways people use their money and figuring out how to make it land in your pocket? You are going to turn a dream into a sellable product, make sure the team is going the right way, and deal with all business stats to make sure the judges are impressed by your team.

### Which one or ones are you? The best Hack teams have a mix of all three.
