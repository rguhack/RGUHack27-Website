The Ultimate Hack List: Must-Have Items at RGUHack

Recommendations from the team on what to bring to the hack

Packing for a hackathon can be a confusing ordeal, especially if it is your first one. Do you bring a change of outfit? A game to play for chill time? Snacks to get you through it? It can be an overwhelming experience. Not sure where to start? Don’t worry, we have you covered. Check out the list below for must-have items people always forget to improve your hack experience!

Basic Hygiene Items

Toothbrush/toothpaste, deodorant, painkillers and lots of water!

Every morning of an RGU Hackathon there is a scamble (even within the committee!) to find the legendary few that remembered to bring deodorant and toothpaste.
When you pitch your ideas at the end of the event you want to feel clean and refreshed, and these items go a long way in boosting your confidence.
Headaches and pains are often common, so please also remember a water bottle and some painkillers (and to occasionally look away from your screens) to help prevent yourself from feeling unwell.

Comfy Clothes

Elasticated waists, joggies, baggie hoodies, trainers, etc.

You will be sat down over a laptop for an insane amount of time during a hackathon and no amount of calming walks and stretches will successfully counteract the evil of a pair of skinny denim jeans! We get this is a networking event and you will want to look nice but please do not sacrifice your comfort for a 5-minute talk with a potential placement company.

Nap Time Items

Blankets, pillows, jammies, noise-cancelling headphones, etc.

It is unrealistic (and not recommended!) to try to stay awake the full hackathon. Being lethargic and tired will make you cranky and stressed when this event is all about having fun! So, bring along whatever you need to hunker down and take a refreshing nap before you need to present your ideas. We have a quiet room each year to provide a comfortable sleeping space so that when you do your pitch you are well rested and ready to go.

Relaxing items: games, books, snacks, etc.

You will not be able to spend every waking minute working on your prototype. Taking breaks in-between sessions are incredibly important so bring along something to keep you entertained and to distract you from your upcoming deadline! We run small workshops throughout the hack to help with this and will have a snack table stocked to keep you going through the hack.

And finally, most obviously… your laptop to build the best prototypes!

Don’t forget your laptop and charger! They are definitely THE most important item needed at a hackathon. How else will you show off your incredible skills and ideas without them?
