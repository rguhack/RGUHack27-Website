The generosity of our sponsors is what keeps this event running

Interested in sponsoring the hack?

Get in touch with us at RGUHack@rgu.ac.uk and we can set up a time to have a chat.

What does my company get from sponsoring the hack?

- Gold sponsors:

  • Opportunity to set an innovative and creative challenge for participants.
  • Longer introduction speech during introduction presentation.
  • All silver/bronze perks.

- Silver sponsors:

  • Opportunity to run workshops on any topic during the event*.
  • Short introdution speech as introduction presentation.
  • all bronze perks.

- All sponsors:

  • Opportunities to connect with future talent from within the University and beyond.
  • Extensive recognition and branding before, during and after the event.
  • Social media promotions, event announcements, verbal mentions.
  • Continued visibility via photos and social media after the event.
